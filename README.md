## The monologue (105 words)

> Come in, come in — mind the third step, it sighs. Yes, I am the Dark Lord. No, the cape is not dramatic; it is warm, and the castle is freezing, and the heating minions unionised in spring. The prophecy says a hero will end me at dawn. Dawn was four hours ago, and three of you have already knocked — one asked for directions, one wanted a selfie, one brought a résumé. I have conquered nine realms, and none of them had decent tea. Sit down, hero. The throne is taken; I claimed it first, and it is the only chair with a cushion.
